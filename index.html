Example projects

Estimating indicators of cyanobacterial harmful algal blooms in New York State

Savoy, P., Gorney, R. M., & Graham, J. L. (2025).

Ecological Indicators, 173, 113403

Cyanobacteria harmful algal blooms (cyanoHABs) are a growing concern for for both environmental and human health, but most states lack funding for monitoring. We designed models to predict algal concentrations and cyanoHABS across 110 New York State lakes and identified a simple low-cost measure that was as effective as costlier laboratory measures for predicting cyanoHABs.

Model development

I develop models to better understand the natural world. Through iterative testing, interpretation, and refinement we can both gain new insights and improve model accuracy.

Data harmonization

My work relies on compiling large multi-source public datasets of climate data, remote sensing, land use, site conditions, and field measurements. I’ve produced publicly available datasets totaling over 80 million records.

Programmatic workflows

I build robust and well documented code to help me gather datasets, develop models, create data visualizations, and communicate results to collaborators and stakeholders.

Statistics

I use a variety of statistical approaches to identify and interpret complex environmental processes. I'm experienced in analysis of both time series and spatial data.

Data visualization

Data visualization and graphic design have been interests of mine since grad school. I enjoy making clear, intuitive, and understandable graphics to explain complex ideas.
